My method: Alignment

I don't work on symptoms.I work on the person.

On your nature, your choices, your energy. That's where lasting results come from.

Where I start

First, I understand how you're wired.

I build your Functional Profile: how you make decisions, when your energy rises and when it leaves you, what recharges you and what drains you.

Then, on that foundation, I design your nutrition, your training and your lifestyle. That's why they hold up even when life tries to throw you off track.

The three pillars of my method

Mind. Body. Spirit.

Three pillars. One person: you.

  1. Mind

    01

    How you make decisions, how you handle stress and pressure, where your attention ends up every day.

  2. Body

    02

    Nutrition, training, sleep. Measured, not guessed: the numbers show you where you're losing energy.

  3. Spirit

    03

    Your nature, the unique way you're wired, what sets the direction for everything else.

Measured, not guessed

Before I change anything, I measure it.

  • 01

    Sleep and recovery

    How much you sleep and how much you really recover, night after night. If you wear a tracker, I read its data too.

  • 02

    Body composition

    Tape measure, photos and mirror call the shots. The scales come later, and I only read a bioimpedance scan taken in the morning, on an empty stomach, on the same machine.

  • 03

    Energy through the day

    What time it dips and what switches it off: you note it down for a few days, then I go through it with you.

  • 04

    Medical tests, when needed

    The doctors I work with request them and assess them. I bring their recommendations into your plan.

You see the first tangible results in three weeks: steadier energy, sleep that restores you, a clearer head. That's the standard I work to.
