How I work with you

One step at a time, never alone.

I work with only a few people, and closely. There's never a day when I lose sight of you.

  1. 01

    The consultation

    Forty-five minutes on Zoom. You tell me where you stand, and I show you where I'd start with you.

  2. 02

    Your Functional Profile

    Questionnaires and measurements, no guesswork. First I understand how you're wired, then I tell you what to do.

  3. 03

    The tailored plan

    Nutrition, training and lifestyle that fit into your diary, with the app and the exercise videos.

  4. 04

    Ongoing support

    I stay close: I measure your progress and adjust course as you go. When your situation calls for it, I bring in the doctors I work with.

The first tangible results arrive in three weeks: steadier energy, sleep that restores you, a clearer head.

What's in the programme with me

Everything you need, nothing more.

  • The nutrition plan

    Meal by meal, with quantities, built around your real days. Restaurant days included.

  • Training

    In the app, with the exercise videos. And I correct your technique myself, from your videos.

  • Lifestyle

    Sleep, stress, routines, travel: what happens outside the gym, which is almost all of your week.

  • Ongoing support

    Chat, meal photos, Zoom and reports: as close as you want me to be.

We decide together how much support you need, in the consultation, with your diary open.

Getting started

We start with a full month, with me beside you. At the end, you decide whether to carry on.
